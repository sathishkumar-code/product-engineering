# Bug Intake — Conversation Questions

Ask these before drafting `bug-report-template.md`. A bug report written from a
one-line complaint ("the report list is broken") is rarely reproducible by anyone
else later — these questions exist to prevent that.

1. Which product and environment — SAL or SNF, production or staging?
2. What did you expect to happen?
3. What actually happened?
4. Walk me through exactly what you did, step by step, right before it happened.
5. Does it happen every time, or intermittently? If intermittent, under what
   conditions have you noticed it?
6. Who or how many residents/facilities/users are affected?
7. Any error messages, screenshots, or logs you can share?
8. Do you have a guess at the cause, or did this appear alongside another change?

If severity or frequency is unclear from the answers, ask directly rather than
guessing a value for the report's Severity/Frequency fields.
