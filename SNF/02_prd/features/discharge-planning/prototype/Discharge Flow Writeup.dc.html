Pending Sign — physician signature loop

Steps 1 and 4 both send documents out to the attending physician for signature. On the physician's phone these land in a Pending Sign queue — each item tagged by type (e.g. Advanced Care Directive, Medication List) and sender. Opening one shows the full document; the physician taps Add Sign, then Submit. The signed status flows back into the SNF workflow, unblocking the relevant step.

  • Queue view: list of documents awaiting this physician's signature, with resident name, document type, and requested date.
  • Detail view: renders the document (medication summary, order form) and offers Add Sign + Submit.
  • On submit, the item is marked signed and the SNF sees the updated status (e.g. “Approved by Physician”).
